# Marketing

## Brand
Independent consumer brand. No personal brand as the primary identity.

The product should feel modern, premium, approachable and international.

Brand name is not final.

## Core marketing message
Working hypothesis:

> A beautiful, simple way to understand where your money goes and how your wealth grows.

Alternative themes:
- Your money, beautifully organized.
- Know where your money goes.
- Control your spending. Understand your money.

Final messaging requires validation.

## Main acquisition channels
### TikTok / Instagram Reels
Primary organic channel hypothesis.

Content can be produced without showing the creator's face:
- app screen recordings
- motion graphics
- AI voiceover
- captions
- generated visuals

Content themes:
- personal finance tips
- spending breakdowns
- saving/investing examples
- app demonstrations
- build-in-public updates
- before/after comparisons
- short product videos

### ASO
Important once the iOS app launches.

Research:
- keywords
- screenshots
- app title/subtitle
- description
- localization
- ratings/reviews

### SEO
Potential future channel for evergreen personal finance content and tools.

## Growth hypothesis
The product itself should create shareable visual moments, especially charts and financial summaries.

Potential loop:
content → install → use app → see useful/beautiful result → share → new user.

## Marketing principles
- Show the product instead of relying on generic advertising.
- Test multiple creatives.
- Use analytics to measure acquisition quality.
- Internationalize content alongside the app.
