# Competitors

## Primary references

### Monefy
**Role:** reference for simplicity and quick expense entry.

Strengths:
- Very simple mental model.
- Fast manual expense entry.
- Clear focus.

Weakness/opportunity:
- The product experience can feel visually dated or less premium compared with newer fintech products.

Our lesson: keep the simplicity, improve the visual experience.

### Margen
**Role:** primary visual reference.

Strengths:
- Strong visual identity.
- Premium interface.
- Animations.
- Strong presentation of net worth.
- Merchant logos/icons.
- Modern fintech feeling.

Our lesson: use the quality bar as inspiration, but keep a much narrower product scope.

### Soldi
**Role:** secondary competitor.

Useful for understanding the feature expectations of a modern expense tracker: accounts, movements, recurring transactions, analysis and imports.

Risk: copying its breadth would move the product away from the desired simplicity.

### Wallet
**Role:** broader category competitor.

Strengths:
- Broad personal finance feature set.
- Mature product expectations.
- Budgeting, accounts, investments and other financial management features.

Our strategy: do not compete on feature count.

### Registro Contable: Gastos
**Role:** reference for the simpler/manual expense tracking segment.

Our opportunity: offer the same basic utility with a substantially more modern and premium UX.

## Positioning hypothesis

**Monefy simplicity + Margen-level visual ambition, with a deliberately narrow scope.**

The key hypothesis to validate:
> Users who want simple manual expense tracking may prefer a beautiful, modern experience without the complexity of a full financial-management suite.
